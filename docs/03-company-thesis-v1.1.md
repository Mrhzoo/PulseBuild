# PULSEBUILD — Vision & Business Model · v1.1
SME project intelligence for UAE & Saudi construction · DOCUMENT 03

An intelligence layer for the 90% of the Gulf construction supply chain that enterprise platforms ignore.

## 1. Vision

Every small and mid-sized contractor or supplier on a UAE or Saudi project should know, by morning, what threatens their cash, crew, or margin this week — without chasing the main contractor, without living inside a heavy ERP, and without guessing from a WhatsApp group.

PulseBuild becomes the default project pulse for the lower tiers of complex construction systems in the Gulf.

## 2. Category we are creating

Not construction ERP. Not BIM. Not another chatbot. Category: **SME project-risk intelligence** — a multi-agent coordination assistant that translates the chaos of large multi-party projects into a ranked cash-and-schedule signal for the companies that actually execute the work.

## 3. Why this can be unique

- Built bottom-up for SMEs, not sold downward from owner tools.
- Multi-agent reasoning across schedule + cash + change (Compliance in v1.5) at once.
- Designed for Gulf communication reality: WhatsApp, email, PDFs, mixed Arabic/English.
- Honest about incomplete data. Confidence is a feature.
- Paid from the first pilot. The product exists to protect money, so it should cost money.

## 4. Who we sell to first

Specialized subcontractors (MEP, finishing, façade, civil packages) and material/equipment suppliers with 3–8 active projects, typically 20–200 people, operating in **Dubai and Abu Dhabi first**.

**v1.1 geo lock:** incorporate and bill in the **UAE first (AED)**. Expand to Riyadh and Eastern Province (SAR billing entity as needed) only after **~10 paying UAE companies** and a repeatable digest demo.

Avoid one-person firms and avoid main contractors for the first wave.

Buyer: owner, commercial manager, or operations manager who feels delayed IPCs and surprise variations.

## 5. Offer and pricing

Monthly subscription per company, tiered by number of active projects monitored. Suggested early range: **AED 1,500–3,500 per month** (SAR equivalent only when KSA entity is live). 30-day paid pilot (discounted is fine; free is not). Annual prepay with 1–2 months included after proof.

Price against avoided delay and protected margin, not feature count. One early change-order seen a week sooner can pay for a year.

Project-pack add-on if a tenant uploads huge archives (protects LLM margin).

## 6. Why a customer pays

- See cash risk early (payment language, retention, delay hitting in 7–14 days).
- Stop surprise variations (scope/qty shifts in documents before the argument).
- One morning briefing (ranked digest instead of hunting WhatsApp/email).
- A record, not a rumor (flag “this affects us” with timestamp + evidence).
- **Optional Reader seat** so a colleague can see the digest without edit rights.

## 7. Go-to-market wedge

- Warm introductions and direct conversations. No ads until message is proven.
- 30–40 discovery talks focused on the last cash-flow or change-order wound.
- 12–15 paid pilots. Deliver digest even if part of the pipeline uses the **assisted-ops lane** (time-capped).
- Convert those who can point to one early warning. Those words become the only sales asset.
- Expand city by city: Dubai/Abu Dhabi → then Riyadh and Eastern Province.

## 8. Unit economics (direction)

Gross margin healthy after LLM cost. Target: model + hosting under 20% of monthly fee at normal usage. Huge archives → project-pack add-on. Sales founder-led until 20–30 paying companies.

## 9. Competition — honest map

Enterprise suites (Procore, Autodesk, large ERPs) win owners and main contractors — not priced for the SME layer. Informal tools (WhatsApp, Excel, paper) are the real competitor. PulseBuild does not replace WhatsApp; it reads the mess and returns judgment. Local consultancies can copy a dashboard; they rarely ship a standing multi-agent loop with a paid digest.

## 10. Risks we accept and how we manage them

| Risk | Management |
| --- | --- |
| Trust barrier | Paid pilot + single success question; evidence not magic |
| Messy data | Incomplete-input design; first-upload coaching; matching + reassign |
| Thin SME margins | Monthly, cancel-friendly; value in 14 days |
| Relationship industry | Founder sells; case language in customer words |
| Privacy / residency | Tenant isolation; no training on files; **one region now**, UAE residency when a payer requires |
| Scope creep into ERP | Written non-goals; no accounting/BIM |
| Assisted-ops becoming consulting | 30 min/tenant/day cap; audit; exit after S5 |
| Hallucination | No number without source; confidence; human flag |
| WhatsApp access delay | Email is the SLA channel |

## 11. What good looks like

10 paying SME companies (UAE first). At least half open the digest three days out of five. At least three can name a specific risk they saw first in PulseBuild. Monthly revenue covers model cost and hosting with room left. No custom one-off builds that trap the team. Golden-set Act evidence rate ≥ 95%.

## 12. Positioning lines

- See the risk to your cash flow before it hits your account.
- The system that watches the big project so you don’t have to chase everyone.
- Built for the 90% of the supply chain that the big platforms ignore.
- Project Pulse for the people who actually do the work.

## 13. Legal / operating posture

Incorporate in a jurisdiction that makes **UAE sales practical first** (UAE mainland or free-zone entity). KSA entity when expanding. Contracts: subscription terms, DPA, no warranty that findings are complete. Customers keep their own commercial records. PulseBuild is decision support.
